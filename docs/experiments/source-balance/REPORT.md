# Equal-provider temporal training

**RESEARCH_CANDIDATE: numeric/provider/reused-source guards pass; independent confirmation and strict inherited-fold audit pending**

This study was registered after longer training and OOD gates failed transfer checks on previously used Metrica3. It tests a change to training, not another threshold on that match. All 40previously screened games are research now; no independent final test was opened.

The previous sampler gave original games 1/2 and SkillCorner 1/2. Since the original family has seven DFL and two Metrica games, Metrica received 1/9 of training draws. This experiment instead samples DFL/Metrica/SkillCorner each 1/3, uniformly over training games/scenarios within each provider. Provider identity affects the sampler only, never inference features.

All registered fits keep the 48-channel, 25-second TCN and use 4500 fixed AdamW updates, the same loss, optimizer, normalization and augmentations as the longer baseline. Train three fixed seeds on five complete neural folds. This isolates provider sampling from the earlier longer-training family; seed trajectories change with the new sampler.

Candidate policies are three individual uniform50 fusions with v3 and their equal-probability ensemble. Select the best fixed-development candidate passing evaluation-1.0 plus the separately preregistered provider guard: primary mean spellF1 must not fall by >0.01 on DFL 7, Metrica 2 or SkillCorner 20 separately. Freeze one candidate before full fitting and reused-source checks; no runner-up is chosen afterward.
